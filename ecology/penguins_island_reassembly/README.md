# Palmer penguins as an island-ecology study

Status: **exploratory biological follow-up**. This directory is intentionally
separate from the frozen ODSP N2 evidence and does not change any N2 endpoint.

## Biological question

Do neighboring Antarctic islands maintain persistent local penguin phenotypes,
or is archipelago-scale functional differentiation generated mainly by
**species turnover plus temporally labile within-species states**?

The ODSP N2 result supplied the clue: morphology predicts island against a
pooled reference, but that gain disappears after species identity is credited.
Here that statistical reversal is recast as an ecological assembly question.

## Current result

The pinned Palmer Penguins raw data contain 152 Adélie rows across Biscoe,
Dream and Torgersen over 2007/08--2009/10. Restricting the analysis to Adélie
provides an internal control for species turnover.

The exploratory model sequence is:

1. trait ~ sex + year + egg date + clutch completion
2. + island
3. + island x year

The fixed island component is small for most variables. By contrast, island x
year structure is relatively stronger for bill depth, body mass and delta15N.
Across the six measured traits, 2--3 of the three pairwise island contrasts
reverse sign across years.

A deliberately simple leave-one-year-out test gives:

- structural morphology (bill length, bill depth, flipper length):
  mean balanced accuracy = **0.342**
- isotopic niche (delta15N, delta13C):
  mean balanced accuracy = **0.340**
- three-island reference = **0.333**

So a phenotype learned from two years carries essentially no persistent island
signature into the third year.

## Ecological interpretation

The useful interpretation is not "islands do not matter." It is more specific:

> Archipelago-scale trait structure is mostly an assembly-level property
> generated by which penguin species occupy an island, while the residual
> within-Adélie island phenotype is weak and temporally unstable over this
> three-year window.

This separates two island processes that are often conflated:

- **between-species sorting / turnover** among islands;
- **within-species local differentiation** among islands.

The current data favor the first process for the large morphology-to-island
signal. They do not support a persistent local Adélie ecotype.

## Important boundary

The island x year pattern is not evidence of individual plasticity. Different
adults were sampled among years, structural traits are largely fixed in adults,
and stable-isotope values integrate pre-breeding foraging rather than local
summer foraging around each island. The pattern can therefore arise from
demographic sorting, sampling variation, phenological state, or environmental
effects on body condition and trophic history.

## Why this is island ecology

Biscoe, Dream and Torgersen are not merely labels for a classification task.
They are neighboring breeding islands embedded in the same regional marine
system. That makes the design useful for separating:

- regional forcing shared by islands;
- island-specific filters;
- colonization and species replacement;
- the persistence or turnover of functional trait composition.

The strongest next step is therefore a **long-term island reassembly test**,
not additional tuning of the three-year classifier.

## Long-term extension

Join annual colony abundance for Adélie, chinstrap and gentoo penguins to the
three species' trait distributions. For each island-year, calculate:

- species composition and turnover;
- community-weighted trait centroid;
- functional dispersion;
- the fraction of trait change attributable to abundance/species turnover;
- where repeated individual measurements exist, the residual within-species
  trait contribution.

The primary ecological test becomes:

> Does long-term functional change on Antarctic breeding islands occur mainly
> through species replacement rather than persistent within-species
> morphological shifts?

A second layer can then relate turnover to regional sea ice and island-level
breeding habitat/phenology.

## Reproduce

Download the pinned `penguins_raw.csv` from palmerpenguins commit
`8957207b78d6ccd1b4654a9dd9c9041b657478ab`, then run:

```bash
python ecology/penguins_island_reassembly/analyze.py \
  --raw /path/to/penguins_raw.csv \
  --out build/penguin_island_reassembly.json
```

No current ODSP manuscript receipt is read or modified by this script.

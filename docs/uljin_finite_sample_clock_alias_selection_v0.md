# What finite-sample clock aliasing can and cannot make a predictor select

**New source-free synthetic route, NOT an Uljin animal effect or qualified
inferential procedure.** Pre-result freeze is in the companion contract.
It follows PR #234, which already disclosed oracle effects, including the
larger smooth-evening synthetic profile. This finite-sample screen is
prospectively frozen relative to its *own simulated outcomes*, not a fresh
blind choice of biological profiles.

## Inferential object

With the 41 original date pairs, 82 simulated physical stations (40 train,
42 held out; stratified across two synthetic region labels), and the same
invariant solar-phase Poisson rate on both branches, draw every synthetic
observation once. Every event has one original site, one original date pair,
one branch, and one solar phase. Transform the **same event** into its
corresponding civil-clock time using its original paired astronomical date.

For complete hypothetical 24h operation, both representations have four
hours of their respective exposure per bin, so both models have a zero
log-offset. This would NOT hold for partially operational real camera
intervals. Conditional binomial models distinguish:

- Null: one branch effect shared by all six 4h bins;
- Alternative: six branch effects with the previously frozen ridge toward
  their mean (lambda=2, intercept ridge=1e-6).

The actual existing ODSP conditional fitter is used on six bin-wise
sufficient statistics. Equality to its full original station×pair-cell fit
is independently tested; original empty cells are retained in the fixed
41×6 cells/site scoring denominator. Only independent whole synthetic
sites are held out. Each region label is a stratum, NOT a replicated
independent ecological region.

The **descriptive decision** is gain>0 for the shape model on heldout
physical stations. Record how often clock-shape is selected and solar-shape
is not. This is an *operational wrong-model-selection frequency* given
the artificial truth world, NOT a calibrated alpha-level test of hysteresis.

## Locked panel and limitations

Three disclosed profiles (uniform, smooth morning, smooth evening) × three
expected total counts (462, 4,623 and 46,230) × 200 independently seeded
worlds = 1,800 worlds. All station-by-pair assignment includes sites with
zero detections. Whole synthetic operational frame is always available;
published 4,623 events do not establish that many events on real 41 date
pairs, so count scenarios are purely hypothetical. Sites have equal
intensity, no station-level ecological heterogeneity, and simulated
timestamps follow ideal original astronomy without device clock error.

The output must report **all nine scenarios**, unconditional clock-only
selection, separate clock and solar selection, Monte Carlo standard error,
mean heldout site-equal gain and median paired gain. Negative, zero and
null results are retained. No after-outcome threshold, bin, profile,
replicate count or penalty changes within this v0. Even a positive
clock-only fraction is not evidence of real false discoveries.

uptime intervals, timestamps, station-independent roster provenance and
within-UJ1/UJ2 physical-block independence.

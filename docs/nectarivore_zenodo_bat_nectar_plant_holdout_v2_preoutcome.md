## Scientific target and prior art
Original paper Thiel et al. (2024), *American Journal of Botany* `10.1002/ajb2.16303`: the authors already report **increased bat and hummingbird visits in midstory** and **correlation with flower availability and nectar quantity/quality**. ## Fixed predictive models and scores

The original camera source documents `Hours` as actual camera operating hours and `Inflorescences` as number of available inflorescences. **Both arms** condition on the same strictly positive source opportunities `e_{g,z} = Hours_{g,z} × Inflorescences_{g,z}`. This offset is a *predeclared working model of encounter exposure*, not independently calibrated species-specific detection efficiency, plant encounter probability or author activity-rate definition; since `Capture_rate` was author defined by Hours alone, the additional Inflorescences offset is an explicit biological *availability* modeling assumption.

Let `a_low=0`, `a_middle, a_high` source-layer log multipliers fitted from training plants. B0 baseline:
`P0_g(z) = e_gz exp(a_z) / Σ_{k∈Z_g} e_gk exp(a_k)`.

B1 new nectar-level proxy:
`P1_g(z) = e_gz exp(a_z + β x_gz) / Σ_{k∈Z_g} e_gk exp(a_k + β x_gk)`,
where `x_gz = log1p(mean original Nect_quant_mean across year observations for this (plant,z))`. **No data-derived scaling, other resource variables or per-layer effect models**. Both arms learn their own baseline a parameters in the training partition, not from heldout plant visits. The scale `log1p` is predeclared with the original nectar quantity units; positive resource assumed and source shall be strictly finite >=0.

Fit by maximizing mean **per-training-plant** count-weighted log score `Σ_z n_gz log P(z) / Σ_z n_gz`; each training plant has equal weight. Deterministic fixed grid: `a_middle, a_high∈[-2,-1.5,-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1,1.5,2]`, `β∈[-2,-1.5,-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1,1.5,2]`; B0 β=0. B1 β learned jointly with a on the same 9x9x9 grid, with fixed objective subtraction **0.02×β²** (per training plant) to discourage overfitting. B0 fit uses β=0; penalties for a terms are not applied to either model. Tie-break exact by smaller `|β|`, then smaller `|a_middle|+|a_high|`, then signed parameter ascending. No flexible response transform, spline, threshold, randomizing plant ID, or post-outcome grid search.

Evaluate leave-one-original-`ML_ID`-out (9 folds). Each target plant's **all observed height strata and visit counts** are entirely excluded from training. Both B0 and B1 are assessed on identical heldout plant × height cells and identical target visit denominators; compute plant-level mean heldout `Σ_z n_gz log P(z)/Σ_z n_gz`, then equal-plant mean gain `Δ=B1−B0` nats per recorded Bat visit. Report absolute B0/B1, each heldout plant gain (ID redacted in public aggregate), number of favorable plants, equal-plant mean, and source plant bootstrap 95% percentile from **10,000** equal-plant resamples, seed `20261123`; descriptive only, not independently sampled forests. Classify `all-positive`, `all-nonpositive` or `mixed` with exact zero nonpositive. No p-value from individual counted visits as independent animals.

Mandatory negative controls and falsification limits:
- synthetic known-truth beta=0 equal resource levels ⇒ no B1 information (at most penalty/tie effect).
- synthetic strong resource gradient learned and heldout improves;
- unseen plant y changed must not change train coefficients;
- camera source true zero count must not be dropped; invalid/unoperated source exposure must never be scored;
- original file MD5/SHA256 and keys/strata count must reconcile.
- An independent code audit may verify arithmetic, but original heldout outcome must remain frozen once opened, including null/negative.

## Claims ceiling
A positive leave-plant-out B1 would be an **exploratory, local predictability of observed bat-camera visit-height allocation conditional on source height, camera-hours and flowering opportunities** and originally measured mean nectar quantity. Original authors already reported nectar/visits associations. It is **not** proof of the causal resource selection by bats, species-specific vertical niche, detector q, synchronized year-specific nectar association, independence across forests, or globally superior 3D SDM. A negative result is binding for this source/model and must not be rescued on the same data. Same original liana species in one Peruvian rainforest; n=9 spatial plant clusters, not independent ecosystems.

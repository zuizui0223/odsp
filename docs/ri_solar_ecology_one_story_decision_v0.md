# One ecological story, not five disconnected RI camera-trap papers

Status: INTERNAL interpretation and next-study plan, **not manuscript submission
or independent ecological confirmation**. The Rhode Island original
v2, taxon-standardization, matched-support and four-model parity
audits are all products of the SAME 2018–2023 public source and must not
be counted as independent empirical replications.

## Central substantive question

**What makes a mammal's detected diel-time organization portable across
seasons and sites: species-specific astronomical phase, civil-clock time,
or seasonal ecological context?**

This matters because seasonal differences in time-of-day detections can
arise without flexible animal behavior. Sunrise/sunset geometry changes;
camera activity/detection effort changes; species and habitat support
changes; and the predictive family itself may smear probability across
clock bins.

A serious ecological paper must separate these alternatives rather
than treat a visually striking seasonal curve as a mechanism.

## Why the ecological cause is still unknown

The source is a carnivore-habitat-weighted camera survey, not an
independently randomized sample of all state sites. Repeated
photographs from one camera are not independent activity observations.
Archived source clock offsets, hardware uptime/detection effort by
local hour, weather, nocturnal detection and animal abundance changes
are not independently calibrated. No animal identities are tracked.

In addition, solar-phase histogram bins push forward to fixed clock
bins via a finite stochastic transition M. Such bin mixing itself can
make clock histograms appear more predictive even for genuine
sun-following time allocation. Equal histogram parameter count is
NOT enough to rule out this model-class explanation.

Thus NO causal effect of sunrise/sunset, temperature, prey, predators
or human disturbance is identified here.

## A SINGLE, genuinely new discriminating field test

Freeze a follow-up before any detection events are opened:

1. Sample physical camera sites using a named, auditable sampling
   frame; freeze **original human deployment/retrieval and device
   health/uptime logs** independent of animal photographs.
2. Verify clock time zone and DST correction using synchronized field
   clock checks, not inference from detected animal rhythms.
3. Define independently measurable ecological predictors:
   ambient temperature, vegetation/visibility, moon illumination,
   prey availability and human disturbance if available.
4. Compare site-heldout and later-year continuous circular time
   densities with direct clock and solar mappings. Apply the
   correct phase-change Jacobian to evaluate both on the SAME
   civil-clock outcome, and use TRAINING-ONLY calibration to match
   effective representational flexibility, not just the nominal
   number of bins.
5. Include both season-specific models, the independent wrong-sun
   control, and controls for observed camera operational time.
6. Use the whole physical site as the sampling/uncertainty unit.
   Predeclare actual site-availability and power gates BEFORE
   species/time outcomes are opened.

Only if solar predictors add transportable information beyond a
capacity-matched season-aware clock model with independently
calibrated effort can the new ecological hypothesis gain
credible support. Even then the result supports *prediction*,
not causal photoperiod entrainment without an intervention or
valid natural experiment.

## Publication decision

Current Rhode Island results are a valuable NEGATIVE/PARITY AND
MODEL-AUDIT STORY, not multiple positive ecological discoveries.
A methods-oriented case study could responsibly report the
solar-vs-clock estimand dependence, sampling composition and
projection restriction, but would need independent demonstrations
of broader impact to justify a general methods claim.

For a mechanism-driven ecological paper, priority is a genuinely
new independent study with operational exposure and environmental
drivers. Do not turn five post-outcome views of one dataset into
five nominally independent papers, and do not rescue the original
negative all-season solar finding via selective post hoc reweighting.

The original ODSP qualified training-process mean and
source-resampling routes remain intact and are separate from this
nonconfirmatory Rhode Island ecological research program.

# Training-process directional transfer inference v1

## Historical status

V1 is the first frozen candidate for inference over a predeclared
training-resampling process. It is retained as methodological provenance, **not
as the active confirmatory method**.

Its prospective null panel passed, but the frozen terminal-power gate failed.
V1 was therefore closed without promotion. Later versions were treated as new
methods with new prospective qualification; the v1 threshold was never relaxed
post hoc.

The active qualified method is v5
(`docs/training_process_positive_cv3two_iut_v5.md`). The active internal and
untouched-external chain is documented in
`docs/training_process_v5_confirmatory_chain.md`.

## Why this is a separate route

The existing all-refit route asks a literal fixed-set question:

> Did every supplied upstream refit support the required positive transfer step?

That is an intersection-union claim about a particular supplied set. It does not
treat those refits as a probability sample and it does not generalize to an
unsampled training process.

The training-process route instead asks:

> If the upstream model is generated by one predeclared training-resampling
> process, is the mean held-out directional gain from that process positive on
> the validation population?

The two claims are intentionally non-equivalent. A process-mean claim can pass
even when one observed refit is negative. That does not rescue the fixed-set
claim; it means the inferential target is different.

## V1 estimand

For validation group g and information step c, let D(r, v; g, c) be the held-out
gain produced by training-process draw r and validation draw v. The v1 target is

```text
E_r E_v D(r, v; g, c).
```

The r expectation is over the **frozen resampling-and-fitting process conditional
on the frozen training source**. It is not uncertainty from drawing an entirely
new ecological training dataset from a larger source population.

The v expectation is over the declared validation population represented by the
validation-block design.

Thus the estimand is not the minimum over refits, the probability that a future
individual refit passes, a statement that all possible refits are positive, or
a population-level claim about every possible original training sample.

## V1 crossed bootstrap

With R observed refits from the frozen process, every bootstrap replicate:

1. resampled R refit identities with replacement once;
2. independently resampled validation blocks within each validation group;
3. shared the same refit resample across every group and information step; and
4. shared each group-specific block resample across selected refits and
   contrasts.

Validation blocks were not redrawn independently for each refit, avoiding an
artificial R-fold multiplication of validation sample size.

V1 used a two-way inclusion-exclusion studentizer with an explicitly
conservative scalar safeguard. Its null behavior was acceptable, but that
conservatism produced insufficient prospective terminal power. The failed power
gate is preserved in the frozen v1 receipts and is not reinterpreted.

## Provenance principle retained by later versions

A numeric refit-by-validation score tensor cannot establish that its rows are
valid draws from the intended process. Confirmatory process inference therefore
requires a pre-outcome process manifest that freezes the empirical training
source and resampling units, stratification rule, refit count, RNG/fit-seed
policy, fitting implementation and training-validation separation rules.

That provenance principle survived into the qualified v5 route and was
strengthened further by managed model generation, whole-source-frame separation,
implementation/runtime locks and managed external scoring.

## Relationship to other ODSP routes

Nothing in the training-process work reclassifies existing evidence:

- fixed-set all-refit intersection remains the method for the claim that every
  supplied refit passes;
- historical nested refit-mixture machinery remains sensitivity-only;
- frozen empirical endpoints remain frozen under their original route;
- failed v1-v4 process candidates remain frozen failures.

The chain that was only an intended target in v1 is now implemented and
qualified, within the narrow v5 scope:

```text
validation-sample uncertainty
    -> upstream training-process uncertainty
    -> managed generation and source-frame provenance
    -> untouched-frozen external validation
```

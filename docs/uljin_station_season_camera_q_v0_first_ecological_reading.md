# Ecological reading of first source-free station×season q result

**Post-first-outcome interpretation only; no frozen analytic method, RNG,
error budget, original 41 matched date pairs or earlier ODSP outputs
were changed.** All numbers are artificial detector/animal event data.

Frozen contract `678bcf2e04620f7a8d856676a20f93482a43d498` predates
first outcome at [dedicated CI 38016397672](https://github.com/zuizui0223/odsp/actions/runs/38016397672).
The first artifact `11656706264` (ZIP SHA256
`db82017465f64877e8b7597525c849f8c310633a4aa8d323a777ad8d87737377`)
contains all 96 paired model comparisons and 192 calibration-arm results.

## Interpret the two detector truths separately

The **branch-invariant but station-heterogeneous** detector world
correctly allows site-only 6 civil 4-hour block q calibration:
source q averaged over rising/falling equals q for each original
branch. Its 48 pair comparisons produced 20 site-majority
certifications under pooled-branch q and 15 under separate
site×branch q, the latter spreading exactly the SAME independently
labeled reference passage budget across twice as many q cells.

The **branch-changing and station-heterogeneous** detector world
makes season-averaged site q a WRONG measurement target for the
original 96 civil-bin observed-event model likelihood:
all 48 paired comparisons are HOLD under the pooled seasonal
source, despite that source sampling scheme being genuine
IID Binomial at its *branch-mean* detector q. Under properly
scoped source station×season q calibration, 13 of 48 source-free
model comparisons certified a majority for A at new IID sites;
35 did not.

## Which models were positively supported under the harder camera world?

All numbers below are outcomes of the frozen synthetic 16-site exact
sign test (four Bonferroni-corrected model pairs), NOT biological
effect-size estimates or evidence for a real animal species.

For branch-changing cameras, **only the larger T=153,600 independent
true-passage calibration budget** enabled:

- Fixed civil-clock animal truth: **mean anchor > solar-phase**
  at both synthetic event counts 20 and 200, 2 comparisons.
- Solar-phase animal truth: **solar-phase > civil clock**
  at both artificial event counts, 2 comparisons.
- Season-shifted latent solar-phase animal truth:
  **solar-phase > civil clock** and **solar noon > civil clock**
  at both event counts (4 comparisons), and **phase+branch >
  invariant solar phase** at both counts (2 comparisons).

At T=38,400, the season-shifted latent truth supported
`solar noon > civil clock` with 20 artificial detections, and
both `solar noon > civil clock` and
`solar-phase > civil clock` with 200 artificial detections
(3 comparisons). Summing gives 13, retaining the complete
unfavorable results.

A nominal prediction winner is NOT a biological causal
*zeitgeber*. A daylength-conditioned spring-versus-fall score
difference remains compatible with temperature history, vegetation,
reproduction, human activity, clock drift, animal route, camera
sensitivity and observational station heterogeneity. The site
majority estimand is only
`P_new_independent_site(D_fixed_A_true_q > D_fixed_B_true_q)>.5`
for the same 82 original dates, conditional on the frozen trained
models, detector reference validity and IID camera/site sampling.
It is NOT confidence in a positive *mean* expected per-site score.

Moreover, q is here constant during all 41 dates of each rising or
falling branch and each civil four-hour bin. Summing individually
date-optimized q bounds is a conservative **OUTER** envelope for
that assumption, not a sharp profile across dates. If true q is
seasonally drifting *within* a branch or clock bin, even the
site×branch source calibration may be invalid and a different
source study with more detailed reference time histories is needed.
